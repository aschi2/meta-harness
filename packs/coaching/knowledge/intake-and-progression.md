## Intake and scope

Record the person's goals, previous activity, practical constraints, preferences and clinician/user-supplied precautions before proposing activities. As a research basis for the fitness example, ACSM's Exercise is Medicine initiative places activity assessment within clinical care and connects people with qualified exercise professionals; it is not an authorization for an agent to provide clinical clearance. Refer health questions and suitability decisions to the treating clinician or a qualified professional. ([ACSM Exercise is Medicine](https://acsm.org/EIM/), accessed 2026-11-03.)

Pack policy: ask what is unknown instead of guessing. The user or clinician must provide excluded tags and approve the library. Do not diagnose, infer precautions from symptoms, infer clearance from silence, or recommend working through pain. Pause affected planning and seek clinician guidance when new health concerns arise. These boundaries are deliberate conservative product policy, not a diagnostic rule or medical advice.

## Progression

The 2026 ACSM position stand reviews resistance training in healthy adults; its population does not establish a safe prescription for a person with a particular health precaution. It reports benefits across different resistance-training approaches, and the accompanying ACSM guidance emphasizes adherence and tailoring to goals and preferences. ([ACSM position stand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/), April 2026, accessed 2026-11-03; [ACSM announcement](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/), 2026-03-17, accessed 2026-11-03.)

Heuristic for this pack: review completed versus planned sessions and user feedback weekly; prefer one small, reversible adjustment within existing limits. Do not apply a fixed percentage progression or infer readiness from completion alone. Do not increase medical risk, relax exclusions, or alter the owner-controlled library. When evidence is insufficient, retain the current plan or propose no change and ask the user. Actual programming and clinical suitability remain with the qualified human; a green checker means only that the encoded constraints passed.

## Generic coaching

The same intake pattern applies to study or skill practice: user-owned goals and resources, bounded sessions, explicit exclusions, logged practice and human-reviewed adjustments. This cross-domain mapping is a design heuristic, not a finding of the exercise literature. Domain-specific professional boundaries must be supplied by the relevant user or professional.
